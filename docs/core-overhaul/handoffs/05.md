## Changed files and behavior

Owned path `packages/ai/src/core/claims/`:

- `extract-claims.ts` implements the frozen `ExtractClaimsV2` signature as `extractClaimsV2`
  and the configurable `createExtractClaimsV2(options)`. It reads only `RunEnvironment`
  ports: `generation` for model output, `clock`, and `audit`. No database global,
  `process.env`, or `fetch` is used.
- `segmentation.ts` deterministically splits every submitted-input snapshot into blocks
  (blank-line paragraphs, table rows) and sentence segments with exact half-open UTF-16
  spans, merging abbreviation and lowercase-continuation breaks. Segments record their
  smallest containing locator, OCR transcription uncertainty, user-caption origin, and a
  language verdict. Chunks own whole paragraphs up to `maxChunkCharacters` (default 6,000)
  and repeat trailing paragraphs of the previous chunk as read-only context
  (`overlapCharacters`, default 1,500).
- `generation.ts` defines the strict chunk-output schema and prompt. The document is sent
  only as `untrustedContent` JSON. The model cites **verbatim quotes plus segment IDs**; it
  never supplies offsets or claim IDs. There is no claim-count cap.
- `validation.ts` accepts a claim only after mechanical checks. Rejections become typed
  `citation_validation_failed` issues plus `validation_rejected` audit events; nothing is
  silently repaired or dropped:
  - every quote must be an exact, unique substring of a segment in the chunk, and at least
    one must lie in a segment the chunk owns; overlapping quotes are rejected;
  - attribution: a direct assertion may not have an attribution cue (`said`, `according to`,
    …) outside its span; attributed claims need an exact attribution quote in a cited
    segment, and `attributedTo` must be written there or in a validated antecedent;
  - negation: cited negation must survive into the claim text, and `negated` must agree with
    both the cited text and the claim text (statement claims embedding a negated clause are
    allowed);
  - time: `statedText` must be written in the cited segment and the claim; a year inside the
    claim span with no stated time is rejected; intervals are computed deterministically
    only for explicit year, month-year, and full dates, otherwise all-null;
  - quantities, units and denominators: `rawText` must appear in the cited text and the
    claim; a stated `of/among/per/out of` denominator cannot be dropped; every number in the
    cited span must survive and no number may be introduced;
  - entities: capitalized tokens in text, subject, object, place, qualifiers, and
    `attributedTo` must be written in the same or preceding paragraph;
  - referents: accepted only when the mention occurs in the cited text, the antecedent quote
    is exact and in the same or preceding paragraph, contains the referent, and a partial
    name does not match two full names there. Otherwise the referent is reverted to the
    mention as written, `unresolvedContext` explains why, checkability becomes
    `needs_context`, and an `ambiguous_input` info issue is recorded. Model-declared
    `unresolvedMentions`, bare-pronoun subjects, and uncertain OCR also force
    `needs_context`.
- `inventory.ts` merges all chunks into one document-wide inventory:
  - claim ID is `claim_` + SHA-256 of the document ID and normalized scoped proposition
    (subject, predicate, object, qualifiers, attribution, negation, quantities, stated time
    and interval, place). `retrievalText` and wording are excluded, so paraphrase text never
    changes identity and IDs are independent of model output order;
  - only identical scope keys merge. The earliest occurrence supplies `spans`; later
    distinct occurrences become `occurrenceSpans`; a repeated extraction of the same
    occurrence from an overlapping chunk is not double counted. An identical proposition in
    a second submitted snapshot is kept with `duplicateOfClaimId`;
  - parent links (for example an attributed proposition under its statement claim) resolve to
    canonical IDs; missing, rejected, self, same-scope, or cyclic parents reject the child;
  - with `maxAnalyzedClaims`, every claim is still returned, ranked material → checkable →
    document order; the remainder is `coverageDisposition: "deferred"` with a
    `deferred_processing` issue and partial status;
  - the coverage audit walks **every substantive segment of each document**, not the
    extracted claims: segments receive linked claim IDs, an explicit
    opinion/background/non_checkable disposition with a reason, or `deferred` (budget
    exhaustion, analysis limit, unsupported language). A segment with none of these is
    omitted: its characters are subtracted from `charactersCovered`, a
    `human_review_required` (or `provider_failure`) issue is emitted, and status is partial.
- `summary.ts` exposes `summarizeInventory`, including `noCheckableClaims` for complete
  inventories with zero factual claims (opinion-only input).
- `evaluation.ts` provides `matchInventoryToGold` and the extraction-only
  `createClaimExtractionAdapter` (`contractVersion: "2.0.0"`, no verdict predictions).
- `fixtures/claim-inventory.dataset.json` and `fixtures/claim-inventory.script.json` hold a
  7-document synthetic dataset (13 synthetic inventory entries, 0 human gold) and scripted
  model output.
- `index.ts` is the `@repo/ai/core/claims` entry point.

The stage emits two `ClaimV2` records with distinct content-derived IDs, spans `[0,52)` and
`[18,51)`, `attributionSpan` `[0,17)`, a year interval for 2025, and
`parentClaimId` linking the attributed proposition to the statement. If `embedded` is instead
labelled `direct_assertion`, it is rejected because the cue “said” lies outside its span.

Downstream obligations:

- Treat `coverageDisposition: "deferred"` claims as inventoried but not analyzed, and skip
  claims with `duplicateOfClaimId` when counting so duplicates are never scored twice.
- `checkability: "needs_context"` claims remain eligible factual claims for scoring; they must
  not become decisive without resolved context.
- An `unavailable` or `partial` extraction result must reach the scorecard as
  `extractionStatus`, which forces a null factual score.

## Security, tenancy, cancellation and audit

Untrusted document text is passed as JSON data, and model output is re-parsed against the
strict schema before use. Discovery hints (URL slugs, link titles, snippets) are never read.
User captions are never sent to the model and are recorded as `background`. Uncertain OCR
cannot produce a `checkable` claim. The stage performs no storage, retrieval or cross-owner
lookup and does not interpret visibility or publication consent; tenant and owner scope stay
with Task 03's ports. Cancellation is checked before the stage and every chunk and is
honored while a generation call is in flight. Audit events cover stage start/finish,
cancellation, budget exhaustion, and each rejection or unresolved referent, using only the run
and snapshot IDs.

Budget usage counts generation attempts as external requests and sums tokens and cost. Unknown
usage stays `null` and is never assumed free. The stage cannot see other stages' consumption,
so Task 11 must pass the remaining allowance and deadline.

## Unresolved blockers and missing capabilities

- Implementation is not blocked.
- No production `GenerationPort` connector exists for claim extraction, so no real model has
  run this stage. Task 11 must supply one; replay and live claim evaluation remain unavailable
  until it does.
- Empirical claim-extraction recall (≥ 0.95) and semantic precision (≥ 0.98) cannot be measured
  without an independently adjudicated human claim inventory.
- Deterministic scope checks are English-only; other languages are explicitly unavailable
  until validated.
- Known conservative behavior: a direct assertion sharing a sentence with an unrelated
  attribution cue, a “not only … but also” construction, or a year belonging to another clause
  can be rejected. This lowers coverage and marks extraction partial; it never accepts a claim
  with lost scope. Measure the rate on real data before tuning.
- Recording dependency: generation usage becomes `null` after a failed call, so the run cost cap
  cannot be enforced by this stage after that point; Task 11's spend reservation remains
  authoritative.
