## Changed files and behavior

Owned path `packages/ai/src/core/scoring/`:

- `score-report.ts` implements the frozen synchronous `ScoreReportV2` signature as
  `scoreReportV2`. It performs no I/O, reads no environment or clock global, and uses the supplied
  `at` instant for zero-duration stage metrics.
- Every claim, decision, assessment, provenance graph, and coverage object is parsed through its
  frozen runtime schema. Duplicate IDs, unknown claims, decisions for duplicate/non-factual claims,
  and dangling, cross-claim, non-validated, or wrong-relation decision citations fail the stage with
  `citation_validation_failed`; none are silently repaired or dropped.
- Only canonical claims with `duplicateOfClaimId === null` and
  `coverageDisposition === "factual_claim"` can enter the denominator. A canonical factual claim
  without a decision is counted as omitted, not relabeled `unverified`; canonical deferred claims
  are counted separately. Duplicate copies receive no weight.
- Verdict counts use only `Decision.publishedLabel`, never `diagnosticLabel`, raw model confidence,
  or calibrated correctness. The formula is exactly
  `100 * supported / (supported + contradicted)`, with equal weight per eligible canonical claim.
  `mixed`, `misleading`, and `unverified` never receive fractional truth values.
- The frozen null reasons are derived deterministically: no eligible claims, zero resolved
  denominator, non-complete input/extraction, resolution coverage below the exact `0.8` threshold,
  a material open mixed/misleading claim, and a failed upstream input/extraction stage. Null reasons
  force a null score through the frozen `scorecardSchema`.
- Evidence summary fields are counts, not an arbitrary quality percentage: admitted snapshot IDs,
  assessment validation states, and independent/unknown dependency groups remain separate.
  Temporal applicability remains on each immutable assessment and is not converted to a recency
  penalty. Provenance graph/root/unresolved chronology/inaccessible-original counts remain separate
  and do not change factual score.
- `index.ts` exports the stage. `fixtures/scoring-invariants.json` freezes eight synthetic cases and
  records zero human-gold claims.

Owned path `packages/ai/src/core/framing/`:

- `presentation-findings.ts` creates stable content-derived presentation-finding IDs. Text-observed
  `emotional_language`, `attributed_quotation`, and `negative_reporting` findings must cite spans
  contained in the submitted claim spans and cannot cite evidence. They never change a verdict or
  score. Attributed statements produce a neutral descriptive finding; negative subject matter and
  attributed quotations receive no automatic penalty.
- Evidence-backed `material_context_omission` and `material_skew` findings require one or more
  `validated`, fully applicable `context` assessments for the same claim. A published `misleading`
  decision produces a material-skew finding from its exact submitted claim spans and corrective
  context IDs. Unknown, cross-claim, rejected, non-context, or out-of-scope evidence fails closed.
- No loaded-word regex, tone-to-falsehood mapping, recency penalty, domain-trust mutation, or omnibus
  weighted average exists in v2. The legacy v1 fallback remains untouched behind the v1 boundary.
- `types.ts` separates text-observed and evidence-backed proposal types; `index.ts` exports the
  validated pure builder.

## Security, scope, visibility, cancellation, and audit

network, provider, `process.env`, global clock, audit sink, tenant lookup, owner lookup, visibility
change, or publication operation. Task 03 and the Task 11 orchestrator remain responsible for
supplying only tenant/owner/visibility-scoped persisted artifacts. This task cannot share content,
change consent, mutate domain reputation, reserve spend, or execute untrusted instructions.

Cancellation is handled by Task 11 before this synchronous stage. A canceled run must retain the
frozen no-scorecard report rule; Task 11 must not call scoring to manufacture a canceled scorecard.
Scoring has no external call or asynchronous interval in which cancellation can be lost. The
orchestrator records the frozen stage outcome and audit event; Task 10 adds no second audit channel.
